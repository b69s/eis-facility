# **Facility Development Plan 2026–2028**

**Supporting Student Learning, Wellbeing and a Safe School Environment**

## **1\. Purpose**

The Facility Development Plan provides a structured overview of the school’s ongoing efforts to maintain, improve and develop learning environments across the campus.

Facility development is considered in relation to student learning, safety, wellbeing, functionality and the long-term needs of the school community. The plan records improvements already completed, projects currently underway and longer-term development priorities.

The plan forms part of the school’s wider continuous improvement process and will be reviewed regularly to ensure that facility development continues to respond to the needs of students, staff and the school community.

A dedicated Facility Improvement webpage provides photographs and visual evidence of completed and ongoing projects.

---

## **2\. Message from the Head of School**

The quality of our physical environment plays an important role in the experience of our students and staff. During the past year, we have undertaken significant improvement work across our campus, ranging from classroom and shared-space upgrades to new student facilities, learning spaces and safety improvements.

Our approach is not simply to renovate facilities, but to consider how our physical environment can better support learning, student wellbeing, safety and a strong sense of community. Some projects have already been completed, others are currently underway, and several longer-term priorities have been identified for the coming years.

This plan brings these developments together within one framework and provides a basis for monitoring our progress and planning future investment.

**Head of School**

# **7\. Monitoring and Review**

Facility development will form part of the school’s wider continuous improvement and annual planning processes.

| Review Process | Focus |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Ongoing Project Review** | Monitor projects currently underway and address operational or implementation issues as required. |
| **Annual Facility Review** | Review the condition, safety, functionality and educational suitability of school facilities and identify emerging needs. |
| **Annual Planning Process** | Review completed projects, determine priorities for the following academic year and consider longer-term development needs. |
| **Leadership Review** | Consider facility priorities alongside student learning, wellbeing, safeguarding, operational requirements and available resources. |
| **Accreditation & School Improvement Review** | Consider how facility developments support wider school improvement priorities and accreditation expectations. |

The Facility Development Plan will remain a living document and will be updated as projects are completed, priorities change or new needs are identified.

# **9\. Continuing Commitment**

Facility improvement is an ongoing process rather than a single programme of renovation. The school will continue to review its physical environment and prioritize developments that strengthen student learning, wellbeing, safety and the overall quality of the school experience.

Through systematic planning, regular review and continued investment, the school aims to ensure that its facilities continue to evolve alongside the needs of its students, staff and community.
